Discovery should change your mind. If it doesn't, it wasn't discovery — it was theatre with sticky notes.

## The tell

You leave with the same solution you walked in with, a thicker slide deck, and a quote for Phase Two. Everyone feels productive. Nobody is safer.

## Real discovery

Asks harder questions about users, constraints, and whether software is the intervention at all. It produces decisions you can defend — including "don't build this."

## How to buy it

Pay for outcomes you can inspect: problem statements that survive contact with users, options with trade-offs, and a clear recommendation. Not for timeboxes that exist to keep a vendor warm.
