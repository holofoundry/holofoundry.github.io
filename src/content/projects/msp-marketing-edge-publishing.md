Marketing operations for multi-client agencies often starts as a spreadsheet and ends as a liability. Version confusion, copy-paste errors, and a publishing calendar that only one person understands.

## What we built

We built a **role-based social content operations platform** for MSP Marketing Edge: centralised publishing, per-client customisation, and enough automation that the team could run the machine without living inside a workbook.

## Design choices

- Clear roles so the right people edit, approve, and publish.
- Per-client customisation without forking the whole process.
- Automation where it reduces error — not where it hides accountability.

## Outcome

A publishing engine the team can own. The spreadsheet didn't need more columns. It needed a product.
